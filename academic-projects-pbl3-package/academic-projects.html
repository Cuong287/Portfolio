PBL 3 · Structural Analysis + Reinforced Concrete Design

Reinforced Concrete Building Design

5-Storey School Building · Da Nang, Vietnam

Structural design of a five-storey reinforced-concrete school building, focusing on the analysis and design of the transverse frame at Grid 3. The project covered preliminary member sizing, load determination, structural modelling, frame analysis, wind-load assessment, reinforced-concrete beam and column design, and reinforcement selection.

02

Engineering Scope

01Structural Scheme

Defined the structural system and preliminary dimensions of slabs, beams, and columns based on geometry, span arrangement, load-transfer behaviour, and constructability.

02Load Determination

Calculated permanent and imposed loads for typical floors, corridors, and roof areas, then converted slab loads into equivalent frame loads for structural analysis.

03Wind Load Assessment

Determined wind actions for the building in Da Nang, including wall and roof aerodynamic effects, and converted the resulting actions into distributed and concentrated loads for the analysed transverse frame.

04ETABS Modelling & Analysis

Developed the structural frame model in ETABS, assigned member sections and loading cases, and evaluated the structural response under gravity and lateral loading.

  • Load transfer through the frame
  • Bending-moment distribution and column axial forces
  • Frame behaviour and global deformation under wind loading
05Reinforced Concrete Design

Designed the main beams and columns using governing internal-force combinations obtained from the structural analysis.

  • Longitudinal and shear reinforcement for beams
  • Column reinforcement and reinforcement-ratio checks
  • Anchorage and lap-splice considerations
