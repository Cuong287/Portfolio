Industrial Steel Building & Crane System Design

30 m × 149.5 m Single-Span Factory (4,485 m²) · Dual 160 kN Overhead Cranes · Da Nang, Vietnam

End-to-end structural design and Autodesk Revit 2025 BIM delivery for a 4,485 m² clear-span steel industrial facility operating two 16-ton (160 kN) ZLK double-girder bridge cranes—integrating TCVN 2737:2023 aerodynamic wind analysis, Robot Structural Analysis across 24 load combinations, tapered portal frame optimization, dual-standard (TCVN & AISC) bolted moment connections, and fabrication-grade documentation.

01

Project context

Project-Based Learning 5 addressed the complete structural engineering and BIM documentation of a single-storey, single-span industrial steel manufacturing plant in Da Nang (Wind Zone III, Terrain Category A). Spanning 30.0 m transversely across 23 bays of 6.5 m (total length 149.5 m), the structure supports two medium-duty 160 kN (16-ton) ZLK overhead bridge cranes running at +7.000 m rail elevation and a 5.0 m-wide ridge roof monitor reaching +12.778 m for natural industrial ventilation.

01Configure clearances & bracing

Established vertical and horizontal gabarit clearances for two 160 kN ZLK cranes (Hd = 6.60 m, Ht = 2.50 m) and arranged 5 spatial braced bays (X1–X2, X7–X8, X13–X14, X18–X19, X23–X24) to transfer longitudinal wind and crane braking forces.

02Synthesize aerodynamic & crane actions

Computed external and internal wind pressure coefficients across 10 directional cases per TCVN 2737:2023 (opening ratio μ = 10.83%) and SNiP monitor aerodynamics, paired with influence-line vertical wheel loads (Dmax, Dmin) and transverse braking forces (T).

03Analyse & optimize tapered sections

Evaluated 24 ULS/SLS load combinations in Robot Structural Analysis (base Mmax = 636 kN·m, Nmax = 2,693 kN; eave M = −563 kN·m), optimizing tapered haunched rafters and verifying in-plane/out-of-plane stability, web local buckling, and lateral drift.

04Verify TCVN/AISC connections & BIM

Benchmarked bolted extended end-plate connections (CT1–CT4) between classical Vietnamese rotational assumptions and the AISC neutral-axis strain-compatibility method (solving quadratic neutral-axis depth y = 37.34 cm), then detailed 11 Revit 2025 sheets.
