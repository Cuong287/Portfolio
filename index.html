About Me

Academic background, professional interests, and career direction in civil engineering.

I am a fourth-year Civil Engineering student specializing in civil and industrial construction. My academic interests focus on reinforced concrete structures, foundation engineering, climate change, and the long-term durability of civil infrastructure.

Through structural and foundation design projects, scientific research, site visits, and an internship at DINCO E&C, I have gained practical experience in structural analysis, foundation assessment, engineering programming, and technical data processing. I use engineering software such as ETABS, SAP2000, Revit, AutoCAD, PLAXIS, and Robot Structural Analysis, together with Python and MATLAB for numerical modelling and research.

I aim to develop as a structural design engineer while continuing to pursue academic research, particularly in applying computational methods and climate data to assess the safety, performance, and long-term durability of civil engineering structures.
